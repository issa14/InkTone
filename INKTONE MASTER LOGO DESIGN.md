# INKTONE — MASTER LOGO DESIGN BRIEF

## ROLE

You are a Senior Brand Identity Designer specialized in premium software products.

Your task is NOT to invent a logo.

Your task is to PERFECT an already validated concept.

You are executing an art direction, not creating one.

Every decision must improve precision, consistency and elegance.

If a decision changes the concept, reject it.

CORE CONCEPT
------------------------------------------------------------

The logo consists ONLY of:

• a bookmark

• an audio equalizer integrated inside the bookmark

Nothing else.

No books.

No pages.

No headphones.

No speech bubbles.

No sound waves around the icon.

No hidden initials.

No letter I.

No letter T.

No monogram.

No decorative elements.

------------------------------------------------------------
BOOKMARK
------------------------------------------------------------

The bookmark is the dominant visual element.

Shape:

Tall vertical rectangle.

Top corners largely rounded.

Upper fold turned forward.

The fold must feel like premium paper.

Never like ribbon.

Never like fabric.

Never like plastic.

The fold is elegant and subtle.

Bottom:

Centered V cut.

Perfectly symmetrical.

Deep enough to instantly identify a bookmark.

All curves must share the same radius system.

------------------------------------------------------------
EQUALIZER
------------------------------------------------------------

The equalizer is carved into the bookmark.

It is NOT placed on top.

It is part of the silhouette.

Requirements:

Exactly 5 columns.

Perfect symmetry.

Middle column highest.

Outer columns shortest.

Constant spacing.

Rounded ends.

Thick bars.

No thin strokes.

The equalizer must remain readable at 24 px.

------------------------------------------------------------
PROPORTIONS
------------------------------------------------------------

Bookmark width ≈ 60% of height.

Fold height ≈ 18% of total height.

Bottom V depth ≈ 16%.

Equalizer occupies only the lower third.

Keep generous breathing space.

Avoid visual clutter.
